SRU Global v28

Key changes: unified Customers & Vendors party master; Billing & Documents combines purchase bills, expenses, salary and supporting documents; Employees & Payroll; Reports; invoice generation repaired with customer GST, printable invoice and automatic stock creation/adjustment; public catalogue reads portal stock state and displays In stock/Out of stock; reorder level remains editable.

Business model: Party = who you deal with. Account = where money is held/owed. Product = what you sell/buy. Stock = how many units exist. Invoice/Bill = source document. Transaction = money movement. Stock movement = quantity movement. Audit = who changed what and when.

For production, move the same logical model into a transactional database; CSV remains an import/export mechanism.
